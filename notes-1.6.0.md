## Dead Grid 1.6.0: the homestead

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

### What's new
- **Crop beds.** Place them right in your yard and choose what each one grows: potatoes, beans, corn, kale or herbs (which make medicine). Crops take water, change with the seasons, and only kale grows in winter.
- **Farmers tend them.** Each farmer or Harvester looks after 3 beds, walks out to them, and picks what's ripe. Untended beds grow slowly, and you can harvest by hand any time.
- **Scarecrows.** Crows eat any bed without a scarecrow within 30 m.
- **Soil.** Planting the same crop repeatedly wears the soil out. Rotate crops, or build a compost heap, which also speeds growth and eats corpses.
- **More ways to make food:** chicken coops lay eggs, a smokehouse halves spoilage and adds food storage, and a field kitchen lifts morale.
- **Home comforts:** campfires people gather around after dark, tents (+2 beds), storage sheds, rain barrels, washstands, benches and flower pots.
- **Build closer together.** Homestead pieces can sit side by side, and you stay in build mode so you can lay out rows. They're quiet to build, so gardening won't draw the horde.
- **Homestead panel** (More → Homestead): your beds, estimated food per minute, spare hands, wilting crops, exposed beds and a Harvest all button.

### Fixes
- The "Food running out" alert now opens the Homestead panel, and new alerts flag wilting crops, crows and ripe beds nobody is picking.
- Crop beds count toward how many farmers can work at full speed.

### Tested
- In a desktop browser: placement checks, every new building on the map, the panels, and about 250 in-game seconds of growth and harvests: no script errors. Winter, crows and soil loss over a long game weren't played through.
- The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check. It has not been run on a physical phone.
