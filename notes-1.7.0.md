## Dead Grid 1.7.0: grit

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

### What's new
- **A new look.** Rust, worn metal, stencilled headings, hazard stripes and a film of grime across the whole game, with a dirtier, sepia map.
- **Bespoke people.** Survivors now differ in build, height, coat, scarf and pack on the map and in the bunker, on top of their unique faces. Raiders wear their gang's colours, masks and scars.
- **Raiders live in compounds.** Every gang is now a real settlement of named people. They scavenge, eat, recruit, and build tents, walls, watchtowers and plots you can see on the map. They expand with new camps and attack constantly.
- **They're worse at it than you.** No robots, no drones, thin income and impulsive spending. They scout badly, sometimes hit a guarded target, and sometimes empty their whole camp for a raid, leaving it exposed. When they starve, people desert, and some beg at your gate.
- **Raiders are people.** Every one you kill is gone from their roster, and survivors of a raid carry their loot home. Tap a camp for a scout report with their faces, numbers, buildings and who's out raiding. Destroying a camp can bring a surrender, and wiping out the roster ends the gang.
- **Survivors grow and change, Sims-style.** Everyone has an age, birthdays, a life goal, a daily wish and a life story in their profile. Completing a goal earns a lasting perk such as Master, Devoted, Veteran, Sentinel or Provider. Hard times leave marks too: Hardened after too many funerals, Broken after long despair, Survivor if they recover.
- **Their bodies keep the score.** Wounds leave scars, grief and age turn hair grey, hunger thins people out, beards grow. The very old can die in their sleep.

### Fixes
- The blank fortifications bar on bandit camps now shows a number.

### Tested
- In a desktop browser: simulated about nine days with two hostile gangs, watching raids, recruiting and deaths, with no script errors. Goals, perks, scars, grey hair and recovery were triggered directly, and all survived a save and reload. Raider balance and real long-game life progression haven't been played through.
- The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check. It has not been run on a physical phone.
