## Dead Grid 1.3.1: trailer and update checks in the app

Android `.apk` only.

### What's new
- **The trailer plays the first time you open the game.** It's built into the app, so it works offline. Tap Skip any time. You can watch it again from the start screen or More → About.
- **About & updates menu** (start screen, or More → About): shows your version, checks for updates, and has links to the website, tips & tricks, the changelog and bug reports.
- **Automatic update check** once a day when the game opens. If a new version is out, a banner on the start screen links straight to the download, which installs over your current version and keeps your save.

### Fixes
- Website links from inside the app now open in your phone's browser instead of replacing the game.

### Tested
- In a desktop browser: the first-launch trailer, Skip, it not replaying on the next launch, the About panel, the update check against the live website, and the update banner.
- The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check. It has not been run on a physical phone.
