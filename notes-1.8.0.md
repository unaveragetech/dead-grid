## Dead Grid 1.8.0: the living world

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

**This changes how the game plays. It's much harder. Sitting behind a wall of snipers at 4× now gets you killed.**

### What's new
- **Guns need crews.** Crew comes from guards, wall crew and fighting robots. Guns without crew fire at a third of their speed.
- **The horde adapts.** If one weapon type does most of your killing, the next night hardens against it and sends what counters it.
- **New infected:** crawlers, armored riot dead, bloaters that explode on walls and guns, screamers that call more, stalkers that climb the wall, lobbers that shell your guns from beyond the wall, and dog packs by day.
- **Nights have a character:** swarm, climbers, bloat, armored, screamers, and blood moons every tenth night.
- **Three-way fights.** The dead attack bandits, and bandits shoot the dead, your people, your robots and your guns.
- **Firefights and hand to hand.** Bandits fight as riflemen, rushers, molotov throwers and looters. Survivors fight back at close range with whatever they carry.
- **Hand weapons:** craft spiked bats, machetes, pistols, shotguns, hunting rifles and SMGs in the Armory. Guards get the best first.
- **New defenses:** Grenade Launcher, Minigun, Claymore, Tar Pit and Flare Mast.
- **Smarter gangs:** they hit your weakest wall section, hold grudges, seize blocks after overrunning an outpost, fight each other and get overrun themselves.
- **Territory matters.** Blocks produce what's on them, such as medicine from a pharmacy block, at full rate if anchored by an outpost, gun or fortified building.
- **Buildings remember.** Paint marks show red X for picked clean, yellow slash for partly searched and green dot for untouched. Tap one to order a search, or fortify and garrison it on your land.
- **The world moves without you:** horde migrations, gang wars, supply plane crashes you race for, spreading building fires, air strikes, outbreaks in gang camps, refugees with the dead behind them. See More → The grid.
- **Big moments drop 4× to 1×.** You can turn that off in The grid.

### Fixes
- **Infection spreads far more slowly**, and killing the dead pushes it back.

### Tested
- Simulated a hands-off 16-sniper base (overrun on night 7) and a simple scripted player with mixed guns who kept building (reached night 16). No script errors. Every world event was triggered. These are first-pass numbers: no human has played this balance. The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check, but has not been run on a physical phone.
