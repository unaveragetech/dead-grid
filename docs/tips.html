🌅 Surviving your first night

Follow the survival guide once

It walks you through the real controls: the wall, your first MG Turret, a well pump and your people. You can skip it, but it's worth doing once.

Watch the clock

The panel at the top left counts down to dusk. Everything you build has to be finished by then, and building takes your crew time.

Three defenses first

Your first objective is to build 3 defenses, and it pays scrap. Two MG Turrets and a Sniper Nest is a solid opening for most maps.

Turn up the speed

The ⏩ button at the top right cycles 1×, 2× and 4×. Use 4× on quiet afternoons and drop back to 1× when the siege starts.

💧 Water & supplies

Water runs out first

People drink every minute. Build a Well pump on day one. It needs 1 power and has to sit inside your wall.

Pipe it home

A well outside the wall needs Water pipes laid back to the HQ, each within 24 m of the last. Pipes are cheap (5 scrap) but fragile, so keep runs short.

Rain is free water

When it rains, your rain catchers fill up. That's a good time to spend water on a scrub-down.

Watch your power budget

Wells, floodlights, tesla coils and every robot use power. Build a Generator (+12 power, burns fuel) or upgrade the Power plant before adding more.

Storeroom before hoarding

Supplies have a cap. The Storeroom upgrade adds +50% to every supply, so a good drone run doesn't go to waste.

Fuel is a winter problem

When the radio warns that winter is coming, stock fuel or build wood stoves. Heating everyone burns fuel every minute.

🧱 Defense

Mix ranges

Sniper Nests (130 m) thin the crowd early, MG Turrets (55 m) handle the bulk, and Flamers (38 m, splash) hold the gate. A single tower type always has a gap.

Floodlights are a multiplier

Towers in range of a Floodlight do +40% damage at night. One light in the middle of a turret cluster beats one more turret.

Barricades and mines shape the path

Barricades soak 320 damage for 15 scrap. Put mines (90 splash damage) where the horde bunches up against them.

Check the wall ring

Open Walls: each segment is a section, green is solid and red is about to break. Use the emergency patch on the four weakest sections before a big night.

Workshop for everything

Each Workshop level adds +20% tower damage and makes repairs cheaper. It's often worth more than another gun.

🔊 Noise

Noise brings the dead

The Noise meter under the clock fills up when you build, fire guns, raise walls or burn bodies. A high meter pulls infected from further away.

It fades on its own

Noise drains a little every tick. Do your loud work (walls, upgrades, construction) early in the day, so it has calmed down by dusk.

Snipers are loud, flamers less so

Every shot makes noise at the tower. A ring of heavy guns makes the next wave bigger as well as killing this one.

Burning the dead is a trade

It clears 15 corpses for 3 fuel, which helps hygiene and disease, but the smoke makes noise. Burn in the morning, not at dusk.

🧍 Survivors

Watch the alert chips

When something needs you (low ammo, a bite, a breach, an idle survivor), a chip appears under the clock. Tap it to jump straight to the fix.

No one should be idle

"1 idle: give them jobs" means someone is wasting a day. Assign them in People → Jobs.

Set your rules early

People → Rules decides what happens to the bitten, to newcomers, to rationing and to ammo. The defaults are safe. Change them when you know your camp.

Morale is a resource

Low morale leads to fights. Keep people fed, washed and rested, and settle disputes fairly. Favouring the same person every time builds grudges.

Talk to people

Tap 💬 in the people list, or walk up to someone in the bunker and tap them. Their memories tell you what's bothering them.

Keep the yard clear at night

If infected get inside the wall, anyone out in the yard can be hurt or bitten. Patch breaches fast, and keep turrets covering the inside of the gate.

💬 Talking to people

Answer the "wants a word" chip

When someone has something on their mind, it shows up as an alert. These are the conversations that matter: confessions, warnings, people about to leave.

Trust opens doors

Secrets only come out once someone trusts you, roughly past the halfway mark. Gifts, kept promises and comfort build trust. Dismissing people erodes it.

Read the person, not the option

"Toughen up" works on proud, nervy people and wrecks the gentle ones. Jokes land with the funny ones. Watch how they talk and adjust.

Letting someone go solo is a gamble

A survivor who offers to scavenge alone can come back loaded, empty-handed, bitten without telling you, or not at all.

Warnings are worth hearing

Ask a worried survivor to say more. They're often the first to notice a hidden bite.

Low trust plus low mood means they leave

Someone who resents you and is miserable will start packing. Ask them to stay before it's too late, and fix what's making them miserable.

🗺️ Outposts, robots & bandits

Fields before farmers

Each farm field lets two more farmers work at full speed. If food runs short with plenty of farmers, you need fields, not people. Idle survivors go to empty fields on their own.

Start next to the wall

Your first outposts should be on blocks touching the HQ. They're easier to reach, and the HQ's guns cover part of them.

One guard per outpost

Bandits pick the outpost with the fewest robots and no watchtower. One posted robot or a watchtower makes them look elsewhere.

Robot works early

Robot works raise robot capacity by 2 per level and make every robot 15% cheaper. With Swarm protocol on, they pay for themselves.

Camps creep closer

Each new bandit camp is built between their compound and your nearest outpost. Burn the forward camps before they become a ring around you.

Fallen isn't gone

An overrun outpost stops producing, but once the block is clear in daylight your crew retakes it. You can also pay to repair it straight away.

Don't assault a camp with damaged robots

Camp defenders shoot back. Robots below half health stay home, and units retreat when they're badly hurt.

Dilemmas don't repeat

Your camp has dozens of possible arguments and requests, and the same one won't come up again until several others have. Your choices are remembered by the people involved.

📁 Case 0047 (no spoilers)

Keep the radio on at night

Some evidence only comes over the air after dark. Check 📻 Radio when the button pulses.

Keep a drone flying

One piece can only be found by a drone out scavenging.

Earn their trust

Two pieces come from your people. When someone "wants a word" and asks to talk privately, hear them out. Survivors who trust you most come forward first.

Survive to find out

Evidence keeps arriving over the first six days. Check More → Case 0047 to see what you have and what's still missing.

🛸 Drones & robots

Use Smart mode

With drones on Smart, they fetch whatever you're closest to running out of. It's the best default until you need something specific.

Batteries and wear

Drones recharge in the bay and wear out over trips. Worn drones crash more often, and the Drone tech upgrade makes them faster, safer and armed.

Crashed isn't lost

A downed drone stays where it fell until a ground robot brings it home. The Hauler rover is best at this and carries twice the cargo.

Robots never sleep

A Sentry bot fights, hauls and fixes walls through the night. It's often the best use of 80 scrap in your first few days.

Blocks run dry

Every trip strips a block a little more, so later runs go further and take longer. Claim new blocks to open fresh ground.

Heavy Mech for strongholds

The Heavy Mech (380 HP, twin cannons) unlocks after a capture and is your best tool for clearing the next stronghold. It doesn't haul.

📻 Radio

Build trust before big deals

Trust grows with gifts and kept deals. A faction you've helped is far less likely to cheat you on a big mission.

Read the risk

Every mission shows how long it takes and how risky it is. Supply drops pay well, but whoever gets there first keeps the pallet.

Some calls are traps

Hostile groups lie. If a deal looks too good from someone marked hostile, assume it's an ambush.

Radio tower

The upgrade adds +50% block income and more survivor signals, so more people find you.

🗺️ Late game

Claim blocks steadily

Every block you hold pays supplies each day. Build → Claim land expands into neighbouring blocks.

Strongholds unlock weapons

Captures unlock Mortar Pit, Heavy Mech, Tesla Coil and Rocket Pod, in that order. Each one changes how you defend.

Push the perimeter

A bigger wall fits more towers, but new sections start half built. Expand in the morning, never right before dusk.

Mortars hate crowds

A Mortar Pit can't hit anything closer than 45 m, so put it in the middle of the compound and let close-range guns cover the wall.

Plan for tanks

Tanks have 900 HP. Stack rockets, mortars and floodlit snipers on the side they come from, and keep the med bay upgraded so the HQ heals between nights.

Bandits shoot back

After wave 5, road gangs can show up at night. They have range, so use barricades to keep them off your turrets.

☠️ Common ways to lose

Building at dusk

Half-finished towers don't shoot, and the noise from building pulls the wave straight to you.

Forgetting water

Food lasts days, water doesn't. A broken pipe at night can empty your stores by morning.

One side, all guns

The horde comes from every direction. Leave no section of the wall uncovered.

Ignoring a bite

A bitten survivor will turn. Your camp rules handle it if you set them, so don't switch them off without a plan.

Running out of ammo

MGs, snipers, mortars and rockets all use ammo. Upgrade the Armory, which turns scrap into ammo, before you add more guns.

Over-expanding

Every new wall section and block needs defending. Grow when your defenses are ahead, not behind.
