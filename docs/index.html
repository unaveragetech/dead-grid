How a day plays

Scavenge by day. Hold the wall at night.

☀️ Day: build and gather

The streets are quiet. Place turrets inside your wall, pipe water to the HQ, and send drones to loot the real shops and pharmacies around you.

🧍 Run the camp

Survivors need food, water, sleep, hygiene and morale. Give them jobs, set camp rules, settle their fights, and decide what happens to the bitten.

🌙 Night: the siege

At dusk the infected come for your walls: walkers, runners, spitters, brutes and worse. Your wall crew patches breaches while the guns do the rest.

💬 Talk to them

Every survivor has a temperament, a secret, a fear and a way of talking. What you say changes what they do next: confess, steal, leave, fall in love, or walk out into the dark alone.

📁 Case 0047

Something about this outbreak doesn't add up. A numbers station at night, a keycard in a gutter, a body with a hospital band, a horde that all turns to face north. Piece it together.

🗺️ Push outward

Claim the blocks around you, capture strongholds for new weapons, answer radio calls, and take the grid back one block at a time.
