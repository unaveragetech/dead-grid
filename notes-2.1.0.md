## Dead Grid 2.1.0: a world that tilts, hums and talks back

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

### What's new
- **Five difficulty modes** on the main menu, from 🧸 Simp-simulation to 💀 Society Implosion. They change how the game plays rather than item prices: how big and tough each night is, how quickly the nights grow, when gangs start raiding and how hard, how often events strike, how likely bites are to turn, how fast gear and the power grid wear out, and how much new survivors trust you. Existing saves play as Survival.
- **A camera that tilts.** The 🎥 button switches between straight down, 25°, 40° and 55°. Streets run back into a hazy horizon, buildings stand up taller as you tilt, and taps still land exactly where you point.
- **Light that follows the clock.** Shadows move with the sun and stretch out at dawn and dusk. Walls are lit on the sunny side and darker at the base, golden hour glows warm, nights turn blue, and the map has a more natural color grade.
- **Conversations about what actually happened.** People bring up last night's fight with the real kill count and the names of the dead, the job they're wasted in, an injury, a feud with someone specific, what you did to strangers on the road, the food running out, or skills they're proud of. Every answer gets a reply written for it, shaped by the person's temperament. Choices are labelled when the person will appreciate them, won't like them, or will remember them. Promises are checked later: say "we'll be ready" and they'll tell you after the next night whether you were.
- **Cars and trucks on every street.** Sedans, pickups, vans, box trucks, buses, police cruisers and ambulances are parked along real roads, some crashed or burnt out. Send someone to search one for fuel, food, medicine, ammo and parts (cruisers sometimes have a shotgun), but car alarms still work. Or have three people push one up to 90 m and leave it as a barricade to block a street or add a layer outside the wall. The dead have to tear through it or crawl past.
- **Powered and dark neighborhoods.** A few substations still run. Within 230 m of one, buildings count as powered, streetlights glow at night, and land you hold there trickles batteries. Substations wear out, faster when the dead crowd them. Tap one to repair it and bring its streets back.
- **Robots that find their way.** Robots slide around buildings instead of getting stuck in them, find a new route when blocked, and wait in a yard outside the HQ door instead of inside it. They have headlights, a hazard beacon, tread marks, a health ring, and a chip showing their job and name.
- **Drones with presence.** They fly higher, with rotor downwash on the ground, blinking navigation lights, a spotlight at night, cargo slung underneath, and their role and battery level shown.
- **Buildings show what they're connected to.** Occupied buildings have a strip of icons for their use, whether they're inside the wall, how water gets there and power. Lines on the map show bucket runs, pipes and generator cables, and a building's card lists every connection.
- **The wall takes in your buildings.** Occupied and garrisoned buildings, and houses the line would cut through when you push it out, are pulled inside the perimeter automatically. Each building also has a "Wall it in" button. Sections that move start half built.
- **New building roles:** 🚚 Supply depot (nearby guns use 30% less ammo), 📡 Broadcast station (draws survivors in, and long guns reach 20% farther), 🚓 Guard post (guards train faster, and an armed cop may sign on) and ✊ Militia hall (everyone fights harder if the dead get inside).
- **Height matters.** Snipers, mortars and other long guns placed beside tall buildings reach 15–30% farther. Placing one tints the rooftops that give a bonus.
- **Eight new camp rules:** Rest rota, Automatic rations, Ammo reserve, Night watch, Scavengers home by dark, Fair shares, Lock down for raiders and Water emergency.

### Fixes
- **The Foreman puts people where they're best.** It now weighs everyone against every job at once, counts talent and traits, and arrivals no longer reshuffle jobs. Before, a skilled scavenger could end up hauling water while a builder stood idle.
- **Menus no longer flicker.** Panels refresh only what changed.
- **The field log no longer covers the place buttons** while you're building.
