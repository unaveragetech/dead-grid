## Dead Grid 2.0.0: a bigger world that remembers

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

### What's new
- **You can see the infected.** Every one now has a dark backing and a color-coded ring, with a clearer health bar. Red arrows at the screen edge count the crowds you can't see yet.
- **Infected catalog** (More → Infected). A portrait, stats, counter-tactics and your kill count for all 12 types. Types you haven't met stay hidden.
- **Move your turrets.** Tap a gun, choose Move, then tap the new spot. It keeps its level and kills, and a gun is offline until the crew has set it back up.
- **Scavenged land shows on the map.** Amber hatching shows how much is left, dark hatching marks stripped blocks, and up close searched buildings get a mark. More → Scavenged has totals and an on/off switch.
- **The map grows.** It pushes out a ring after nights 5, 10, 16, 23, 31 and 40, from 11×11 blocks up to 23×23, using real map data with new buildings, loot and infection.
- **Gangs start small.** They begin as drifting bands, settle into camps around night 6 and can become walled holds after night 16. Size and raids now scale with your camp. Hostile gangs have a type: Desperate, Thieves, Zealots, Deserters or Feral. You can feed Desperate ones.
- **Strangers on the road.** Families, traders, deserters, con artists, pilgrims, medics and mechanics pass through. Help, invite, trade, refuse or rob them. They may come back with a gift or a grudge, and some settle nearby as a new camp.
- **Gear comes from houses.** Scavengers strip cloth, padding, leather, pipe, springs, plate and rare gun parts from buildings, and each building only gives so much. Weapons and armor are limited and wear out.
- **Blueprints and a workbench.** The Armory is now the Workbench, with a blueprint catalog. Weapons must be learned first, by searching buildings, finding a working one or capturing a stronghold. Upgrading the Armory unlocks better recipes.
- **Realistic, upgradeable armor from day 1.** Knee pads become hockey shin guards, a work coat becomes a motorcycle jacket, and so on up to riot gear and plate carriers. Armor reduces damage and can stop bites.
- **People notice who gets what.** Survivors left without gear get sad or angry, lose trust and may confront you. You choose who is equipped first.
- **5 new turrets and 7 new hand weapons,** including the Ballista, Anti-Materiel Rifle, crossbow and LMG.
- **5 new drone roles:** Salvager, Decoy, Spotter, Shocker and Firebug.
- **Conversations with consequences.** 28 branching stories can pay off much later: a dog that warns of attacks or turns on its owner, a lie that comes out at dinner, a cache that may be trapped. Small talk is now assembled from about 400 written fragments, and recent lines are checked so they don't repeat.

### Fixes
- Raids start later and are smaller early on.

### Tested
- Simulated a hands-off sniper base (overrun on night 6 or 7) and a simple scripted player (still alive at night 17 when the test ended, up from night 10), with no script errors. Every conversation branch was walked at random. Map growth was tested in Boston.
