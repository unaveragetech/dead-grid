## Dead Grid 1.9.0: people who pull their weight

Android `.apk`, or play in the browser.

### What's new
- **Real skills.** Everyone has ten skills shaped by who they were: the retired cop shoots, the plumber fixes things, the nurse heals. Skills grow with use and change how well they work and fight.
- **Every feeling has a reason.** Tap anyone to see exactly why they feel how they do, such as "+8 Doing what they're good at" or "-7 Wasted here".
- **People act on their own.** Skilled survivors patch up the wounded, fix guns, shore up the wall, bring in harvests, cook for everyone and comfort each other without being asked.
- **The Foreman.** Jobs are assigned automatically to the best person for what the camp needs right now, scaling up for sickness, filth and thirst. Jobs you set by hand are respected for a while.
- **Standing orders** (People → Rules): Foreman, gate discipline, siege curfew, call to arms, repair crew, burning the dead, drone service, harvest rota and medical stock. All on by default.
- **New Cook job.** Stretches food and lifts mood.
- **Real conversations.** Ask what they're worried about and get an answer from what's actually happening in your camp. Ask what they're good at, thank them, practice with them, give them the day off, or put them on a new job. Options you've used disappear for the day.
- **The wall is no longer a force field.** It's 40% weaker, free repairs are nearly gone, and the dead pile up and climb over each other.
- **The gate.** Open lets scavengers and robots work outside, and lets the dead wander in. Shut is safe, but nothing comes home and people get restless. It opens and shuts by itself unless you take over.
- **Houses are useful.** Occupied buildings can be a Bunkhouse, Workshop, Canteen, Clinic, Lookout or Warehouse, run by your best person for the job. Spare beds now attract new survivors, each with their own skills, and having your own room lifts mood.
- **Everything is explained.** Tap any supply at the top to see where it comes from, how fast it's changing and how to get more. More → How it works covers the rest.
- **Medics can save the bitten.** With medicine, a medic may save someone in quarantine, depending on their skill.

### Fixes
- The siege curfew gets non-fighters in the HQ under cover during attacks.
- The camp now ends when nobody is left alive.

### Tested
- Simulated a hands-off sniper base (overrun on night 7) and a simple scripted player (night 10), with no script errors. The tests caught and fixed a Call to Arms bug that sent the whole camp to die. No human has played this balance, and it may be too hard. The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check, but has not been run on a physical phone.
