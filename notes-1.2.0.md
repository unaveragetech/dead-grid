## Dead Grid 1.2.0: Case 0047

Android `.apk` only.

### What's new
- **Case 0047, the mystery under the outbreak.** Over your first six days, eight pieces of evidence surface. They come from a numbers station on the night radio, a drone find, a body at the gate, a horde that freezes and turns north all at once, and survivors who only talk once they trust you. Track it all in the new **Case file** (More → Case 0047). The beta's story ends on a cliffhanger.
- Evidence arrives with its own cutscene, and some of it comes up in conversation.
- **New beta trailer:** five real cities, the mystery, a tension score, and a very bad night.
- The website's screenshots and trailer now use public landmarks.

### Fixes
- The "perimeter pushed out" message shows a rounded distance instead of a long decimal.
- Cutscenes no longer draw over an open panel.
- The address box's example is now a public landmark.

### Tested
- In a desktop browser: all eight pieces of evidence, both conversation-delivered clues, the case file, a full night, dawn, deaths, recaps and a save reload. No script errors.
- The APK built, signed with the pinned key and passed the version check. It has not been run on a physical phone.
